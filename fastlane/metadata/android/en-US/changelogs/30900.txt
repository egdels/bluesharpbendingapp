What's new in 3.9.0
• New (experimental): the "Basic Pitch" chord engine detects chords, double stops and octaves more reliably
• Chord mode shows the playing technique, e.g. "Octave 1B 4B (tongue block)"
• MPM is the default algorithm for new installs – more precise on bends
• Settings are stored more robustly and carried over automatically
• Fixed: microphone kept running several times after settings changes (battery), flickering chord display, octave errors (Hybrid)
