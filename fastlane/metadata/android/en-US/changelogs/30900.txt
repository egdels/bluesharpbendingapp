What's new in 3.9.0
• New (experimental): the "Basic Pitch" chord engine detects chords, double stops and octaves much more reliably – see "Chord Engine" in the settings
• Chord mode now shows the playing technique, e.g. "Octave 1B 4B (tongue block)"
• MPM is the new default algorithm for new installs – more precise on bends
• Fixed: microphone kept running several times after settings changes (battery), flickering chord display, octave errors in the Hybrid algorithm
